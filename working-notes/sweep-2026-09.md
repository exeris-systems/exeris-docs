This is the finding that most changes what the sweep did. `b2b-technical-whitepaper.md:10` and
`high-level-architecture.md:173` matched `Exeris.RetractedFigures`, but both matches sit inside the
sentence that **withdraws** the figure:

> earlier revisions of this document asserted "up to 60% of CPU cycles" and a ">160 GB allocation on
> a 4 GB payload" figure that no campaign in `exeris-benchmarks` supports. Both are withdrawn.

Under rule 4 that text is preserved verbatim and fenced with the sanctioned inline toggle, which is
what was done. In the HLA the withdrawal lives inside a table cell, so the toggle comments were
placed inline within the cell rather than on their own lines; the table still renders. The elided
report path in the same cell was expanded to the full, checkable
`results/reports/2026-07-21-entity-read-by-id-tuned-pg-triad-comparison-eligible.md`, verified to
exist on `exeris-benchmarks` `origin/main`.

**`exeris-benchmarks/docs/CLAIMS.md` is on that repository's `main`; the local checkout predates it.** The checkout is on
`chore/community-app-kernel-0.7.1`, which predates the file; it exists only on `origin/main`. Four
standards files, this repo's `CLAUDE.md` and the whitepaper all cite it by that path. Every rule-4
judgement in this sweep was made against a verbatim 989-line copy taken from `origin/main`.

## Rulings taken during the sweep

- **ADR-023 obligation 6 — the quarterly audit cadence stands.** The obligation says periodic manual
  licence audits substitute until the registry and CI job land, "performed quarterly". An earlier draft
  of the sweep called that claim false on the ground that no audit had ever run. It was running: this
  sweep is an audit of exactly that kind, and it is the first with a written record. The owner confirmed
  quarterly as the right cadence for a full documentation pass, and named a second natural trigger —
  ahead of a larger release. Nothing in the obligation is edited; adding the release trigger to its text
  would be an amendment and is left open.

- **`adr-conventions` rule 6's status vocabulary is correct as written.** A cluster proposed widening it
  to include `stale`, on the ground that the shared validator accepts that value. The validator checks
  `status` for every document type; rule 6 governs records, and a record does not go stale — it is
  superseded or retracted. The narrower record vocabulary is deliberate, so there is nothing to widen.

## Validator findings judged wrong

1. **`frontmatter_check.py` on `templates/*-TEMPLATE.md`.** The three templates carry a literal
   `last-verified: YYYY-MM-DD` placeholder, which is correct for a template and which the checker
   reports as `last-verified must be YYYY-MM-DD (got 'YYYY-MM-DD')`. It is not reachable in CI —
   the bundle prescribes `extra-paths: "adr rfc *.md"` for this repo, which excludes `templates/` —
   so it surfaces only when templates are linted explicitly. Either the checker exempts
   `*-TEMPLATE.md`, or templates move under a `_`-prefixed directory, or templates stay unlinted
   and the gap is accepted. A maintainer call; nothing was changed to work around it.
